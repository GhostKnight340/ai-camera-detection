Needs hips & knees in frame

  • Standing — upright, knees extended
  • Walking — upright with hip travel
  • Sitting — knees bent, hips at knee height
  • Crouching — knees bent, hips below
  • Bending over — torso tilted forward
  • Lying down — torso near horizontal

Upper body is enough

  • Arms raised — both wrists above shoulders
  • One arm raised
  • Reaching out — elbow straight, arm out
  • Hand to head
  • Leaning — shoulder line tilted
  • Moving / Still — shoulder-centre velocity
Actions are derived from joint geometry. Works with only the upper body in frame — posture actions are suppressed when hips and knees are not visible.
